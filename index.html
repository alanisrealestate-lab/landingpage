Questions

Straight answers.

Is it really just 2%, even if another agent brings the buyer?

Yes. You pay 2% total at closing. That's it. Whether the buyer comes from our marketing or walks in with their own agent, your cost doesn't change.

How do buyer's agents get paid if I'm not paying them?

Through a buyer's premium. The buyer works out their agent's pay with their own agent, and they can roll it into their mortgage. It doesn't come out of your pocket. Agents still get paid, so they still bring buyers.

How can you do full service for 2%?

Because the buyer's agent isn't paid out of your 2%, the whole 2% goes to marketing and selling your home. Same work as a traditional listing. You just aren't paying two agents.

Are there any upfront fees?

None. Photos, video, sign, ads, all of it is on us. You owe nothing unless your home sells.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel anytime, for any reason. No hard feelings. We care more about our reputation than holding anyone hostage. (For the record, we've never been fired.)
